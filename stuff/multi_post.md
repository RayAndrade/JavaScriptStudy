### 1. Hootsuite

**Networks Supported:**

* According to Hootsuite’s integrations list: Facebook, Instagram, X (Twitter), LinkedIn, TikTok, YouTube, Threads, WhatsApp, Pinterest. ([Hootsuite][1])
* So it covers **YouTube**, **Facebook**, **LinkedIn**, **X**, and **Pinterest** — all five of your listed networks, plus more.

**Pricing / Plan Info:**

* Standard (sometimes called Professional) plan: up to ~10 social accounts. ([Capterra][2])
* Example: One source says “Professional plan starts at $99/month” for up to 10 social profiles. ([Capterra][2])
* Enterprise plan: custom pricing for unlimited social accounts/users. ([Hootsuite][3])

**Pros:**

* Broad network support (covers all your networks).
* Strong scheduling + analytics features.
* Good if you want one tool to handle everything.

**Cons:**

* Cost may be higher (especially if you have many accounts/users).
* Might be more features than you need if you’re managing a few personal/creator accounts (vs large team).
* Some features may require higher-tier plans.

---

### 2. Buffer

**Networks Supported:**

* Buffer supports many networks: Facebook, Instagram, X (Twitter), LinkedIn, Pinterest, YouTube, Google Business etc. ([Buffer][4])
* So again, it covers **YouTube**, **Facebook**, **LinkedIn**, **X**, **Pinterest**.

**Pricing / Plan Info:**

* Free plan: Up to 3 “channels” (i.e., social accounts) and 10 scheduled posts per channel. ([Buffer Help Center][5])
* Essentials: $6/month per channel (for paid plan). ([Buffer Help Center][5])
* Team: ~$12/month per channel. ([Late][6])
* Pricing is “per channel” (i.e., per social account) under paid plans. ([Buffer Help Center][5])

**Pros:**

* More affordable for smaller scale creators.
* Supports all your needed networks.
* Easy to start with the free plan if you only have a few accounts.
* Good for solo user/creator rather than large team.

**Cons:**

* Because pricing is per channel, cost can add up if you have many accounts.
* Some advanced features (analytics, large team collaboration) may only be in higher plans.
* Free plan is limited in number of posts & channels.

---

### 3. SocialBee

**Networks Supported:**

* Supports Facebook (pages/groups), Instagram, X (Twitter), LinkedIn (profiles & pages), Pinterest, TikTok, YouTube, Google Business Profile. ([SocialBee][7])
* So covers your needed networks: YouTube, Facebook, LinkedIn, X, Pinterest.

**Pricing / Plan Info:**

* Multiple tiers: e.g., “Bootstrap” (from ~$24/month billed annually) for a starter plan. ([SocialBee][7])
* Pro and higher tiers for more profiles/workspaces/users. ([SocialBee][8])

**Pros:**

* Supports exactly the networks you want.
* Fee seems more moderate compared to large enterprise tools.
* Good mix of features for scheduling, customization, content categories.

**Cons:**

* May have fewer “enterprise” level features than biggest tools.
* Need to check exactly how many accounts/boards per network are allowed in your plan.
* If you manage many accounts across networks, cost might go up.

### My recommendation for *your situation*

Since you have accounts on YouTube, Facebook, LinkedIn, X, and Pinterest, and (from your past context) you seem to be a creator/teacher rather than running a large agency, here’s what I suggest:

* If you want **simplicity + good value**, **Buffer** or **SocialBee** are likely the best fit.
* If budget is tight and you only have a few accounts to manage, Buffer’s free or low-cost plan could work.
* If you anticipate growing (many accounts, many posts, more visual content) maybe SocialBee offers a better balance.
* If you want “all in one” and may expand to bigger teams or need deeper analytics, “Hootsuite” is there — but maybe more than you need right now.
