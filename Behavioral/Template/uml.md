
### **Template Method Pattern (GoF Participants)**

**1. AbstractClass**
• Defines the skeleton of an algorithm — the fixed steps everyone must follow.
• Some steps are handled here, while others are left open for subclasses to fill in.
• Think of it like a recipe outline that says, “These steps are always the same, but you can customize the ingredients.”

**2. ConcreteClass**
• Implements the missing steps defined by the AbstractClass.
• Fills in the details while keeping the overall structure the same.
• It’s like a chef using the same recipe but adding their own seasoning and flair.
